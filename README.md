# long-play

A 24-week personal music education: a syllabus, not a feed.

Most recommenders optimize for the next click. long-play optimizes for the
listener — a sequenced course that transfers what you already know (theory,
listening) onto the music you actually play, plus the history and the "how"
behind it. Five albums a week, each one serving a learning objective. The
terminal goal: read dense, critic-level music writing fluently.

**Design law (the Kraftwerk law):** context unlocks appreciation. Never assign a
challenging album without its story first.

## What's here

- [`syllabus.md`](syllabus.md) — the full 24-week course: units, weekly album
  sets (1 anchor, 3 adventurous, 1 wild card), one concept and one production
  technique per week. Draft v0.1.
- [`weeks/`](weeks/) — the current week's listening assignment, with
  listen-for cues.
- [`docs/curriculum.md`](docs/curriculum.md) — the pedagogical model: why a
  course, how assessment works, the glossary-as-curriculum.
- [`docs/engine.md`](docs/engine.md) — the recommendation engine design:
  candidate generation, scoring, and what production systems and research say.

## Status

Draft. Week 1 is assigned; the listening log and the now-playing sampler that
will feed the engine are still being built. The syllabus will move as the log
shows what lands and what bounces — a bounced wild card is data, not failure.

The repo improves itself: a daily job picks one small, tested improvement
(from sampler data and listener feedback) and pushes it here. Silent unless blocked.
